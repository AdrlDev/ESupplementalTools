The Notes of Note-Re Dame

Anonymous

Once upon a time, there was a little note sitting alone on a line. There really wasn't much excitement in the note's life, no one to talk to and no one to play with. That is why we shall call him SOLO. "[sighs] What a boring place," Solo [sighs] sighed. "It's too quiet around here and there's nothing to do."

While Solo was sitting on his line, a fairy appeared.

"Hi, Solo, why so glum?" the fairy asked.

"Oh, well," Solo replied, "do you know that you are the first person I've seen and talked to in ages? I just feel so alone. I wish life would be more exciting."

"Say no more," the fairy said. "Your wish will soon come true. I feel extra nice today. So, I'll treat you to a pleasant surprise."

The fairy waved her hand. In an instant, Solo and the fairy were transported to a village.

[happily] "[happily] Welcome to the village of Note-Re Dame!" the fairy [shouts] exclaimed.

"This place sure looks lively," Solo said. His spirits began picking up.

"Hope you'll like your stay here," the fairy said. "You'll be meeting a lot of other notes like you. Enjoy yourself. But remember, you must try to get along with the other notes. You may find out it's not so easy living with others." With these words, the fairy vanished.

Solo couldn't quite understand the fairy's parting words. Nevertheless, he shrugged it off He was more excited about meeting the other village notes.

Solo saw other notes playing. They were putting up some bars. Each of them made different sounds, he noticed. Solo came to them and offered his help.

"Thanks." the note with a big voice said. "I am DO, and who are you?"

"My name is Solo and I'm pleased to meet you."

"I'd like you to meet my other friends, too. These are RE, MI, FA, LA, TI. Friends, meet Solo," Do said. "How about calling him Sol for short?"

Now, Sol was happy. He had friends. He felt he belonged to a group. Soon, he joined them in their work and play.

The following months were the happiest for Sol. After a long while, something happened.

All the notes started to fight. Each note wanted his own sound and pitch to be heard. And they argued a lot! There was no harmony among themselves. All they made was noise! Noise! And more noise!

One day, a stranger walked into the village. He was so tired. He decided to sit under a big tree. But after a while, he became so upset. The notes were making a lot of noise.

"Some rest I can get!" he angrily grumbled to himself.

Then the stranger could no longer stand the noise. He approached the little notes.

[seriously] "Listen," he said. "Do you realize that for the past hour, all you've been doing is creating noise?"

"What about it?" Mi retorted. "And who are you anyway?"

"My name is Johann Sebastian Bach. I say, you fellows may have a good thing going if you stop all that noise. Make music instead."

"Music? What is that?" the notes asked.

"Well, music is making sounds that are pleasant to hear," Johann said. "In music, each note contributes his part to come up with a melody that has harmony."

"Harmony?" The notes again looked puzzled.

"Yes, in harmony all the notes get along with one another," Johann said. "Here, let me show you."

Johann took all the notes by hand and arranged them in a sequence. He asked them to sound off their pitch when he told them to.

"Hey! We sound different!" the notes agreed. This was the first time they agreed in a long time!

"Ah, that is harmony. You are no longer fighting to see who is the best!" Johann said. "You are all working together. And you have produced better sounds. We can come up with a little melody that will produce music!"

The little notes realized that it was good to have harmony. Of course, they still had petty quarrels once in a while. But Johann was there to remind them. Johann arranged the notes into something that produced melody or tune. The notes learned to skip and dance around the bars. They made more pleasant sounds. They made music.

The music told stories of love, beauty, and nature. The little notes learned that although each of them was different, each of them was beautiful in his own way. No one was greater than the other. By himself, a note could not produce music. All of them working together could produce beautiful and harmonious melodies.

"Now you can make everybody happy with your music," Johann said as he bade them farewell.

"Thank you, Johann, and come back to visit us." the notes chimed in unison.

Soon after that, news spread far and wide about the notes of Note-Re Dame. Johann did come back many times more. Other composers and musicians came back to the village. Great men like Fredric Chopin, Ludwig van Beethoven, Robert Schumann, Francis Schubert, and Johann Brahms all came to play more music! Oh, what beautiful music were made for all the world to listen to and remember!
