## What's the Message?

6. **Dream vs. Reality**  
   The singer says "They can say it all sounds crazy." Have you ever had a dream that other people thought was crazy? How did you handle it?

7. **Vision of the Future**  
   "A vision of the one I see" and "what the world could be" — What kind of world do you want to make in the future? Why?

8. **Courage to be Different**  
   The line "I don't care, I don't care, so call me crazy" shows bravery. When is it okay to not care what others say about your dreams?

9. **Connection to Graduation**  
   Your caption says this was your elementary graduation song. Why do you think this song is chosen for graduation? What does "a million dreams are keeping me awake" mean for a student moving to the next level?

10. **Designing Your World**  
    "We can live in a world that we design." If you could design your own world, what 3 things would you put in it and why?
