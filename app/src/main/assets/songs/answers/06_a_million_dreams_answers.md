## What's the Message? – Suggested Answers

1. **Dream vs. Reality**  
   Yes, like wanting to be an artist/teacher despite others saying it's hard. I handled it by ignoring negative comments and continuing to practice.

2. **Vision of the Future**  
   I want a world that is kind, peaceful, and equal — where everyone has a chance to learn and follow their dreams.

3. **Courage to be Different**  
   It's okay to not care when your dream is good and doesn't hurt anyone. If you believe in it, you should keep going even if others laugh.

4. **Connection to Graduation**  
   It's chosen for graduation because it is about hope and new beginnings. "A million dreams are keeping me awake" means a student is excited and nervous for the next level, with many goals in mind.

5. **Designing Your World**  
   My 3 things would be:
   - Free education for all — so no child is left behind.
   - No discrimination — everyone is respected.
   - Clean environment — safe future for everyone.
