# The Journey of a Raindrop

Author: Isabella Rose Flores

## Poem

A tiny raindrop in the sky
Looked down upon the earth below.
It saw the trees, the fields, the flowers,
And wished that it could help them grow.
The clouds were dark, the winds were strong,
And soon the raindrop joined the song.
It fell upon a little leaf,
Then slipped into a stream below.
It traveled through the winding river,
Where fish and little turtles swam.
It helped the flowers bloom with life,
And washed away the dust and grime.
The raindrop reached a farmer’s field,
Where seeds were waiting in the ground.
It helped the crops begin to grow,
And made the earth look fresh and round.
The farmer smiled with thankful eyes,
For water helped his plants survive.
The raindrop journeyed to the sea,
Where waves were rolling in the sun.
It mixed with water deep and blue,
And felt its journey was not done.
The sunlight warmed the ocean wide,
And clouds began to form once more.
The raindrop rose into the sky,
And joined the clouds it knew before.
It learned that water moves around,
From earth to sky and back once more.
Its journey showed a lesson true:
All life depends on water too.
So let us keep our rivers clean,
And never waste the water’s gift.
For every drop can help the earth,
And every drop can make a lift.
The water cycle shows us how
The earth keeps living day by day.
