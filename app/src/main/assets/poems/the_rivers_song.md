# The River’s Song

Author: Sofia Mae Villanueva

## Poem

A river flows through fields of green,
Its silver waters shining bright.
It sings a song both soft and clear,
From early dawn to falling night.
The fish swim freely in its stream,
While children watch and quietly dream.
Along its banks, the flowers grow,
And butterflies move through the air.
The trees stand tall beside the water,
Providing shade and beauty there.
The river gives the plants their drink,
And helps the living creatures thrive.
But one day, plastic filled the stream,
And dirty water flowed along.
The fish grew weak, the flowers died,
And no one heard the river’s song.
The children looked with worried eyes,
And knew they had to make things right.
They gathered friends from far and near,
And cleaned the riverbank with care.
They picked up bottles, bags, and cans,
And placed the rubbish where it belonged.
They learned to keep the water clean,
So life could flourish in the stream.
The river sang once more with joy,
Its waters clear beneath the sun.
The children knew that caring hearts
Could help the earth when work was done.
They promised every day to do
Small acts of care for nature too.
