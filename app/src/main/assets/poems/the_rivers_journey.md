# The River's Journey

## Poem

From mountain stones, the river starts,
Carrying stories from distant parts.
It rushes, tumbles, twists, and turns,
Past quiet fields where sunlight burns.
“Whoosh!” it calls as waters race,
Then slows beneath the willow's grace.
It carries leaves, it carries sand,
And travels onward toward the land.
Sometimes the river meets a stone,
A barrier standing all alone.
It does not stop or turn away;
It finds another path to stay.
At last it reaches the ocean wide,
Where countless waves move with the tide.
The river teaches, as it goes,
That changing paths can help us grow.
