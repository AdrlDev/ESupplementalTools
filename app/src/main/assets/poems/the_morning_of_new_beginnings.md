# The Morning of New Beginnings

Author: Hannah Grace Mendoza

## Poem

The morning sun began to rise,
And painted gold across the sky.
The birds sang songs upon the trees,
While gentle clouds went drifting by.
A brand-new day had come again,
With chances waiting in the rain.
A little girl named Ana woke,
And thought about the day ahead.
She had a test at school that day,
And many things were in her head.
She felt afraid she might not know
The answers that she needed most.
Her mother smiled and said to her,
“Believe in all the work you’ve done.
You studied hard and tried your best,
So face the day and have some fun.”
Ana took a breath and smiled,
And walked to school with courage mild.
Inside the classroom, Ana sat,
And read the questions one by one.
She remembered what she had learned,
And slowly found the answers there.
She did not rush, she did not fear,
But worked with patience and sincere care.
When all the papers had been checked,
Ana was proud of what she’d done.
She learned that effort matters more
Than being perfect for everyone.
Each new day gives us room to grow,
And every challenge helps us know.
So greet the morning with a smile,
And welcome every chance to learn.
The future holds a thousand paths,
And every step can help us turn.
For life is full of new beginnings,
And hope is found in every morning.
