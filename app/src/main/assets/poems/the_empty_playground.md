# The Empty Playground

## Poem

The playground waits beneath the sun,
But where are the children who laugh and run?
The swings move gently in the breeze,
While yellow leaves dance beneath the trees.
No shouting echoes across the ground,
No skipping feet make a rhythmic sound.
A lonely ball rests near the gate,
Waiting quietly, patiently, late.
Then suddenly—footsteps! One, two, three!
Children arrive as happy as can be.
The swings rise high, the laughter grows,
And sunlight dances where everyone goes.
The empty playground wasn't sad forever;
Sometimes quiet moments change together.
A place can feel completely new
When someone brings life into the view.
