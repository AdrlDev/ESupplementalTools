# The Kindness Tree

Author: Lucas James Rivera

## Poem

In a little village square,
There stood a tree with branches wide.
Its leaves were green, its roots were strong,
And children played beneath its side.
The tree was loved by everyone,
For shade and beauty in the sun.
One day, a boy named Marco came,
And saw an old man sitting there.
The man looked tired and very sad,
With no one near to show him care.
Marco brought a cup of water,
And offered kindness like a daughter.
The old man smiled and thanked the boy,
Then told him stories from his youth.
He spoke of how a little kindness
Could help the world become more good.
Marco listened with a gentle heart,
And felt a friendship had its start.
The next day, Marco brought a friend,
Who saw a girl with books to carry.
They helped her walk back to her home,
And made her heavy load less weary.
The girl smiled and said, “Thank you,”
And showed them kindness in return.
Soon everyone began to see
That kindness spread from heart to heart.
A simple smile, a helping hand,
Could make a happy, caring start.
The village grew more warm and bright,
As people helped with all their might.
The tree still stands beside the square,
Its branches wide above the ground.
It reminds the people every day
That kindness makes the world go round.
And when we help someone in need,
We plant a seed of love indeed.
he Kindness Tree
