# The Little Seed

Author: Miguel Andres Reyes

## Poem

A little seed beneath the ground
Lay sleeping in the earth so deep,
While raindrops softly sang above,
And roots began their quiet leap.
It had no leaves, no flowers bright,
But held a dream of reaching light.
The morning sun came shining down,
And warmed the soil with gentle rays.
The seed began to stretch and grow,
Through many slow and patient days.
It learned that growing takes some time,
Like climbing up a mountain climb.
A tiny stem appeared one day,
So green and fresh beneath the sun.
The little seed had found its way,
And felt its journey had begun.
Though small and weak, it stood up tall,
And did not fear the winds at all.
The rain would fall, the storms would blow,
The clouds would cover up the sky.
Sometimes the seed would bend so low,
But still it tried its best to rise.
It learned that strength is not just might,
But standing up after a fight.
At last, the little seed became
A lovely tree with branches wide.
It gave cool shade to tired feet,
And birds found homes where they could hide.
Its journey taught a lesson true:
Great things can start with something small and new.

## Multiple Choice

### 1. Where was the little seed sleeping?
- A. In a flower pot
- B. Beneath the ground
- C. On a tree
- D. Inside a house What helped the seed grow?
Answer: B

### 2. Inside a house What helped the seed grow?
- A. Raindrops and sunlight
- B. Toys and books
- C. Wind and snow only
- D. Rocks and sand What did the seed learn about growing?
Answer: A

### 3. Rocks and sand What did the seed learn about growing?
- A. Growing happens instantly.
- B. Growing takes time and patience.
- C. Growing is always easy.
- D. Growing does not need sunlight. What happened when storms came?
Answer: B

### 4. Growing does not need sunlight. What happened when storms came?
- A. The seed stopped growing forever.
- B. The seed tried its best to rise again.
- C. The seed turned into a flower.
- D. The seed disappeared. What is the main lesson of the poem?
Answer: B

### 5. The seed disappeared. What is the main lesson of the poem?
- A. Small beginnings can lead to great things.
- B. Trees do not need rain.
- C. Growing is impossible.
- D. Seeds should stay underground forever.
Answer: A

## True or False

### 1. The little seed began its journey beneath the ground.
Answer: True

### 2. The seed grew into a lovely tree with wide branches.
Answer: True

### 3. The seed learned that growing takes time and patience.
Answer: True

### 4. The seed gave up when the storms came.
Answer: False

### 5. The poem teaches that great things can start from something small.
