# The Little Inventor

Author: Ethan Miguel Garcia

## Poem

A young inventor named Leo
Loved to build and make new things.
He dreamed of flying little cars,
And robots with mechanical wings.
His room was full of tools and toys,
And boxes filled with clever joys.
One day, he made a tiny fan
That turned whenever sunlight came.
He placed it near his window sill,
And watched it spin with gentle flame.
He felt excited by his work,
And thought of what he could explore.
But when he tried to make it spin,
The fan began to shake and fall.
The wires broke, the motor stopped,
And Leo felt upset with all.
He wanted to throw it away,
But then remembered what they say.
“Each mistake can teach us something,”
His teacher said one afternoon.
“An inventor learns by trying,
And keeps on working toward the moon.”
Leo took his tools again,
And tried to fix the fan with care.
He checked the wires, cleaned the parts,
And tested every piece once more.
He learned about the motor’s work,
And how the sunlight made it turn.
At last, the little fan began
To spin and dance beneath the sun.
Leo smiled and felt so proud,
Not just because the fan was new.
He learned that patience, hard work,
And curiosity help dreams come true.
He knew that every great design
Begins with one small, thoughtful try.
