# The Bridge of Tomorrow

Author: Devina Natali Putri

## Poem

Devina Natali Putri
Before the morning painted gold across the sky,
A quiet river whispered as the clouds went by.
Its silver waters carried leaves from shore to shore,
While distant birds called softly, asking nothing more.
Beside the river stood a bridge of weathered stone,
Its arches strong, though years had made them worn.
Each passing footstep made a gentle, hollow sound,
As people crossed from one familiar world to ground.
A young girl stopped and listened to the stream.
She watched the sunlight shimmer like a dream.
The river did not hurry, yet it never stayed;
It moved around the rocks, adjusting as it made.
She thought about the problems she had faced—
The words she could not say, the mistakes she could not erase.
Some days had felt as heavy as the rain,
And some had left behind a quiet ache of pain.
Then came a breeze that stirred the willow tree.
Its leaves danced lightly, wild and free.
The girl took one slow breath and understood:
A difficult road could still lead somewhere good.
The river did not know where it would flow,
Nor could the bridge predict the path below.
But both continued, steady through the day,
Trusting that each step would show another way.
So when the girl walked home beneath the light,
She carried something stronger than the morning bright:
We may not see tomorrow clearly from today,
But courage grows each time we choose to find a way.

## Multiple Choice

### 1. Why does the poet describe the river as “whispered” in the opening lines?
- A. To show that the river was dangerous
- B. To create a quiet and peaceful atmosphere
- C. To explain that the river was very shallow
- D. To show that the river was disappearing 2. What does the river moving around the rocks most likely symbolize?
Answer: B

### 2. To show that the river was disappearing 2. What does the river moving around the rocks most likely symbolize?
- A. Giving up when problems become difficult
- B. Moving quickly without thinking
- C. Adapting when obstacles appear
- D. Avoiding responsibilities 3. What change occurs in the young girl's feelings during the poem?
Answer: C

### 3. Avoiding responsibilities 3. What change occurs in the young girl's feelings during the poem?
- A. She changes from confident to frightened.
- B. She changes from worried to hopeful.
- C. She changes from excited to angry.
- D. She changes from curious to disappointed. 4. Which image best supports the poem's message about perseverance?
Answer: B

### 4. She changes from curious to disappointed. 4. Which image best supports the poem's message about perseverance?
- A. The distant birds calling softly
- B. The sunlight shimmering on the water
- C. The bridge showing signs of age
- D. The river continuing despite the rocks 5. What does the final line, “courage grows each time we choose to find a way,” suggest?
Answer: D

### 5. The river continuing despite the rocks 5. What does the final line, “courage grows each time we choose to find a way,” suggest?
