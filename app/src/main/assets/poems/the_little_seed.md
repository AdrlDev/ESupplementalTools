# The Little Seed

## Poem

Deep in the earth, a small seed lay,
Waiting quietly for the day.
Drip, drip, rain came from the sky,
While warm winds whispered softly by.
A tiny root began to grow,
Down where the hidden shadows flow.
A little shoot reached toward the sun,
Its journey had only just begun.
Through storm and heat, it stood up tall,
It bent with wind but did not fall.
With patient roots and leaves of green,
It became the strongest plant seen.
The seed had learned, beneath the ground,
That growing takes time without a sound.
With hope and care, both small and great,
Can grow beyond their starting state.
