# A Friend Like the Sun

Author: Daniel Matthew Cruz

## Poem

A friend is like the morning sun,
That brings warm light to every day.
When clouds of sadness fill your heart,
A friend can help them fade away.
With gentle words and caring hands,
A friend will help you understand.
A friend will listen when you speak,
And never laugh at what you feel.
They help you find your hidden strength,
And make your broken courage heal.
They stand beside you when you fall,
And remind you that you matter most of all.
A friend will celebrate your joy,
And smile when you achieve your dream.
They will not feel unhappy when
Your success begins to brightly gleam.
They cheer for you with honest pride,
And walk with you from side to side.
Sometimes two friends may disagree,
And words may hurt the heart inside.
But friendship grows when both are willing
To say, “I’m sorry,” and to try.
Forgiveness helps the heart feel free,
And brings back peace and harmony.
So let us be the kind of friends
Who share their kindness every day.
A smile, a helping hand, a word
Can brighten someone’s cloudy way.
For friendship is a precious light,
That makes the world feel warm and bright.

## Multiple Choice

### 1. What is a friend compared to in the poem?
- A. The moon
- B. The morning sun
- C. A star
- D. A cloud
Answer: B

### 2. What does a good friend do when you feel sad?
- A. Ignores you
- B. Laughs at you
- C. Helps you feel better
- D. Leaves you alone
Answer: C

### 3. How does a friend react to your success?
- A. They become angry
- B. They feel jealous.
- C. They celebrate your joy.
- D. They ignore your achievement.
Answer: C

### 4. What can help solve disagreements between friends?
- A. Anger
- B. Forgiveness and saying sorry
- C. Avoiding each other
- D. Blaming others
Answer: B

### 5. What is the main message of the poem?
- A. Friends should always agree.
- B. Friendship is built through kindness, support, and forgiveness.
- C. Friends should compete with each other.
- D. Friends should never talk about problems.
Answer: B

## True or False

### 1. A good friend listens when you speak.
Answer: True

### 2. A good friend laughs when you share your feelings.
Answer: False

### 3. Friends should be happy when their friend succeeds.
Answer: True

### 4. Forgiveness can bring peace and harmony to friendship.
Answer: True

### 5. The poem says that friendship makes the world feel cold and lonely.
Answer: False
