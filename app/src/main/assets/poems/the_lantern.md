# The Lantern

## Poem

A little lantern glowed at night,
A circle of warm and golden light.
Beyond its glow, the darkness stayed,
But inside the circle, shadows faded.
A traveler walked along the road,
Carrying slowly his heavy load.
The path was long, the night was cold,
Yet still the lantern brightly glowed.
It could not make the darkness end,
Nor could it know where the road would bend.
But step by step, the traveler knew
There was enough light to guide him through.
The lantern teaches something small:
We don't need to see the whole road at all.
Sometimes we only need enough light
To take one more step into the night.
