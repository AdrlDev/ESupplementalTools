# The Dream That Grew

Author: Noah Daniel Bautista

## Poem

A little girl named Mia dreamed
Of becoming a doctor someday.
She wanted to help the sick and weak,
And make their pain and fears go away.
She knew the road would not be small,
But she believed she could learn it all.
Each morning, Mia read her books,
And learned about the human body.
She studied bones and muscles too,
And asked her teacher questions freely.
Sometimes the lessons felt so hard,
But still she worked with all her heart.
Her friends would ask her why she tried
So hard to reach a distant goal.
She told them, “Dreams need work and time,
And patience helps the dream grow whole.”
She knew that every little step
Could help her reach the dream she kept.
One day, she saw a sick young child
Who needed care and gentle hands.
She wished that she could help him more,
And understood her dream’s demands.
She learned that helping others well
Was why she wanted this career.
The years went by, and Mia grew,
With knowledge, courage, hope, and grace.
She studied hard and never stopped
Believing in her chosen place.
At last, she became a doctor,
And helped the people of her town.
Her journey taught her friends to see
That dreams are built with work and care.
A wish alone cannot be enough;
We must prepare and do our share.
When we believe and do our best,
Our dreams can grow like seeds at rest.
