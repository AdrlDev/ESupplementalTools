# Stuff You Missed in History Class: Pranks

1. H_A_ is an intentional deception passed off as the truth.
2. _R_N_ is a mischievous practical joke or playful stunt.
3. C_R_E_R_ is the last name of Josiah, Brown University's fictional professor.
4. B_R_E_S is the London street Theodore Hook gridlocked in 1810.
5. _L_U_I_N is a deceptive appearance or trick that misleads the senses.
